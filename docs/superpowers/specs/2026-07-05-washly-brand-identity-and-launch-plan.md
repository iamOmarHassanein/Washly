# Washly — Brand Identity & Social Launch Plan (2026-07-05)

## Decision summary
Full rebrand chosen by owner. Identity: **"Lime porcelain"** — bright, white-based, vibrant, elegant, modern. Target customer: busy young professionals (25–40, downtown Montreal) who pay for convenience. Two recurring humor formats. Mascot kept exactly as-is (no redesign).

## 1. Brand system (rules for every post, reel, story, cover)

### Colors
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Base / background | Pure white | `#FFFFFF` |
| Type / ink | Near-black ink | `#15181A` |
| Accent (the ONLY accent) | Electric lime | `#C9F53C` |
| Soft tint (cards, chips) | Mist green | `#EEF3E4` |
| Secondary text | Warm gray | `#5A6157` |

Rules:
- White is the stage. Lime never covers more than ~15% of a layout.
- Lime is used as: highlighter swipe behind 1–2 key words, pill CTA buttons, the MTL badge, small dots/details. Never as full background (exception: occasional story frames).
- No gradients, no glow, no extra colors. Photos should be bright/airy.

### Typography
- Display / headlines: **Archivo** (weight 800–900), tight, sentence case with a period. ("Your weekend, back.")
- Body / captions: **Inter** (400–600).
- Signature move: the key word of every headline sits on a lime highlighter swipe, slightly rotated (~-1°).

### Logo & handles
- Wordmark: `WASHLY.` in Archivo 900, ink black, with lime period.
- Badge: lime pill with black `MTL`.
- Handle everywhere: `@washly.mtl`. Old glossy green W logo is retired from social (website to be updated later).

### Mascot
- The exact 3D olive-green glossy robot (round head, dark visor, big eyes). Never redesigned, never recolored.
- Generation reference: Higgsfield media_id `f5a07ce3-6a18-4d16-bdcb-d11eeec84ca0`.
- He is the comedian of the brand: expressive poses, props (sunglasses, lemonade, towels, wrinkled shirts), reactions.

### Voice & humor (captions + on-image text)
- Tone: confident, warm, a little cheeky. Short sentences. French/English mix welcome (Montreal).
- Two recurring humor formats:
  1. **Vacation mode** — the joke is freedom: chores are done, everyone relaxes. ("Laundry day, cancelled.")
  2. **Meme callout** — playfully roast the viewer's laundry habits; make people tag friends. ("Still wearing the floor shirt?")
- Every post ends with one clear CTA: book pickup / link in bio / promo code.

## 2. Content pillars (rotation)
1. **Humor / mascot** (vacation mode + meme callout) — reach & shares.
2. **Satisfying process** — folding ASMR, before/after piles→crisp stacks, packaging. TikTok's favorite.
3. **Trust & how-it-works** — 3 steps (Book → We pick up → Fresh in 24h), pricing transparency, insured/tracked.
4. **Offers & conversion** — first-pickup discount, referral, student/newcomer angles.

Cadence target: Instagram 4 posts/week (2 reels, 1 carousel, 1 static) + stories; TikTok 4–7 videos/week (reels cross-posted).

## 3. Launch plan — exact posting order (zero followers)

### Phase 0 — setup (before post 1)
- Create Instagram + TikTok as `@washly.mtl`. Profile pic: lime pill with black `W.` on white. Bio: "Laundry, picked up & back in 24h 🧺 Montréal · Book below". Link to booking site.
- Prepare 9 posts before going live so the grid never looks empty.

### Phase 1 — reveal week (posts 1–5)
1. **Brand reveal static** — mascot waving next to "Montreal, meet Washly." (lime swipe on "Washly."). Caption: what Washly is, in 2 lines.
2. **How-it-works carousel (3 slides)** — Book → We pick up → Fresh in 24h. One icon/mascot pose per slide.
3. **Meme callout static** — "Still wearing the floor shirt?" (the generated post). Ask viewers to tag the friend.
4. **Reel #1 (hook: "POV: you never do laundry again")** — fast cuts: bag pickup, machines, folding, delivery. Trending audio.
5. **Offer static** — "First pickup −25%. Code: WEEKEND." Vacation-mode mascot art.

### Phase 2 — weeks 2–3 (rhythm + proof)
6. Reel #2 — folding ASMR / before-after transformation.
7. Vacation mode static — "Laundry day, cancelled." (the generated post).
8. Carousel — pricing transparency ("What 24h freshness costs").
9. Reel #3 — meme-callout video version (mascot judges a messy room via green-screen).
10. Story series — poll: "Floor shirt or fresh shirt?" + booking link sticker.
11. First testimonial/review post (screenshot style on mist-green card).
12. Reel #4 — "3 things you didn't know about laundry in MTL" (bilingual hooks).

### Phase 3 — week 4 (convert)
13. Referral post — "Give 10$, get 10$."
14. Neighborhood targeting posts — "Plateau, we're coming for your hampers." (rotate boroughs).
15. Re-run the best performer as a paid boost (small budget, MTL 25–40 geo).
16. Podcast/long-form teaser (if pursued): 60-sec clips, white frame + lime captions, mascot reaction stickers.

KPIs: saves + shares (humor posts), completed bookings from link, CPM/CTR on the one boosted post. Review after week 4, double down on best pillar.
